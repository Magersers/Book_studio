RESD / Russian Emotional Speech Dialogs
Authors: Artem Amentes, Ilya Lubenets, Nikita Davidchuk (2022).
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Aniemore/resd
Transcribed release: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Aniemore/resd_annotated
The upstream dataset card declares the MIT license; no separate LICENSE file was published.
Three short human studio recordings are included, resampled to 24 kHz mono PCM16. Source paths, revision, row and original SHA-256 are in manifest.json. Dialogue numbers are not actor identifiers.
